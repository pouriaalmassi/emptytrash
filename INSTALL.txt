Drag Empty Trash.app into Applications.

Before the first launch, add Empty Trash in System Settings > Privacy &
Security > Full Disk Access and enable it (System Preferences > Security &
Privacy > Privacy on macOS Monterey).

Drag the installed app from Applications onto the Dock, or launch it from
Spotlight. Each launch permanently deletes the contents of your Trash and
then quits. There are no confirmation dialogs, windows, or notifications.

The app includes your home Trash and your user Trash on mounted volumes.
Files macOS refuses to delete remain in the Trash. Failures are recorded in
Console under subsystem com.pouriaalmassi.emptytrash.
